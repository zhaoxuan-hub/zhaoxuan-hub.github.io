Zhaoxuan (Zhao-Xuan) Wei

I am a PhD candidate in Operations Research at the Institute of Operations Research and Analytics, National University of Singapore, working with Prof. Andrew Lim and Prof. Hanqin Zhang.

My research applies stochastic modeling and algorithm design to understand principles of decision-making and coordination in operations. I develop analytical and AI-enabled methods for optimizing complex operational systems under informational and structural constraints, with applications in multi-agent systems, inventory management, and spatial logistic service systems.

Research

Research interests

01

Decentralized coordination & learning

Optimizing multi-agent operations through coordination of specialized experts operating in silos via welfare-optimizing multi-agent reinforcement learning.

02

Inventory & supply systems

Inventory and assemble-to-order systems with latent backlogs, stochastic patience, order crossover, and learning under censored observations.

03

Spatial service systems

Joint location and capacity design under proximity–pooling trade-off.

Selected work

Working papers

01

Multi-Agent Queueing Network: Coordination with Learning

with Andrew E.B. Lim and Hanqin Zhang · In preparation for submission to Operations Research

Decentralized coordination via MARL in queueing networks managed by specialized agents with private local models.

02

Learning to Coordinate in Multi-Agent Decision Making

with Andrew E.B. Lim · Working paper

General learning-based coordination methods for multi-agent operations under informational constraints.

03

Inventory System with Impatient Demand and Latent Backlogs

with Andrew E.B. Lim and Hanqin Zhang · Resubmitted to Operations Research

Optimal policies and learning methods for inventory management when waiting customers are impatient and unobserved by the inventory manager.

04

Blessing of Order Crossover in Inventory Systems

with Yunfang Yang · Major Revision at Operations Research

Quantifies the operational value of order crossover in inventory systems with stochastic lead times.

05

Pooling vs. Proximity: Design of Parcel Locker Networks with Endogenous Service Rates

with Quanmeng Wang and Guodong Lyu · Under review at Management Science

Joint location and capacity design for spatial parcel-locker systems with endogenous customer pickup times.

06

The Lyapunov-Function-Based UCB Algorithm

with Andrew E.B. Lim and Hanqin Zhang · Under review at Operations Research Letters

A UCB framework for controlled Markovian systems using Lyapunov-function-based performance analysis.
